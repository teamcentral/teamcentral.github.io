On this page

Settings ​

Settings is your hub for account options, channels, and notification preferences. Open it from Settings in the navigation drawer.

At the top you'll see your name and the email address tied to your account.

Account ​

OptionWhat it does
Notification PreferencesTurn the Daily Summary on or off and set its time and time zone. See Notifications & Daily Summary.
Manage ChannelsCreate, edit, and remove the channels that feed your Pulse feed. See Managing Channels.

Apps ​

OptionWhat it does
Privacy PolicyHow your data is handled.
Usage TermsThe terms that apply to using Corbi.

Time zone

Your time zone is used to time the Daily Summary. You can set it from Notification Preferences — see Time zone.

Signing out ​

You can sign out from the navigation drawer. The next time you open Corbi you'll be asked to sign in again.

Your preferences follow you

Channels, notification settings, and time zone are tied to your account — so they're the same whether you use Corbi in your browser or on the mobile app.
